Our Story

Founded with a passion for automobiles and perfection, khanna Automobile & Car Decor started with a single, clear promise: “In The World Of Promises, We Deliver Quality.”

We realized that car owners often struggle to find genuine audio electronics, wrinkle-free bucket seat fitment, and reliable interior craftsmanship under one roof. We built this studio to eliminate that hassle by offering certified Pioneer sound setups, tailor-made upholstery, and professional vehicle enhancements right here with transparent pricing.

Whether it's installing high-definition Android infotainment screens, high-wattage LED setups, or handcrafting ergonomic leather seats, every vehicle is treated with master-level attention to detail.
